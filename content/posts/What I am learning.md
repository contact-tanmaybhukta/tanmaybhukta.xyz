I’m Tanmay Bhukta. I’m 20, and I’m interested in how creative ideas become useful, sustainable businesses. This is a working map of what I want to understand—not a claim that I have all the answers.

## Cinema and distribution

A film needs a way to find its audience. I’m exploring how positioning, release strategy, marketing, and distribution connect. The question I keep coming back to is: who is this story for, and what helps those people discover it?

## Marketing and creator businesses

An audience is made of people with limited time and attention. I want to learn how creators earn trust, communicate clearly, and build something people value enough to return to. A large audience and a healthy business are different things; understanding that relationship interests me.

## Finance and management

I’m learning to ask practical questions about costs, revenue, cash flow, and execution. What does a project cost to deliver? Which assumptions matter most? How would I know whether an experiment worked? These questions help make an ambitious idea concrete.

## Technology

I’m interested in tools that help people do good work: organizing information, reducing repetitive tasks, and making creative processes easier to manage. I want to start with a specific problem and understand the people experiencing it before choosing a solution.

## How I want to learn

Read carefully. Talk to people doing the work. Try small experiments. Write down the assumptions and the results. Revise my view when the evidence changes.
