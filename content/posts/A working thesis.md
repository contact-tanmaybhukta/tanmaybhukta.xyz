My interests keep meeting in the same place: the relationship between culture, capital, and technology. This is a starting point for my thinking, open to revision.

## Culture gives people a reason to care

Films, creators, and communities shape what people pay attention to. I’m interested in understanding the story, taste, and context behind that attention. What makes an idea meaningful to a particular audience?

## Distribution gives an idea a route to people

Making something and helping it reach people are connected tasks. I want to explore how a clear audience, the right channels, and thoughtful marketing can help creative work travel. Different projects need different routes.

## Capital makes the trade-offs visible

Every project has constraints. Time, money, ownership, and incentives shape what can be made and sustained. I’m learning how to think about those constraints before treating growth as the only goal.

## Technology can make the work easier

Useful tools can improve a process, help a team understand information, or reduce repetitive effort. I’m curious about where they help creative businesses most, and where human judgment remains central.

## The next question

What small problem could I help one filmmaker or creator solve? A limited experiment, with a clear goal and an honest account of the result, seems like a useful place to begin.
